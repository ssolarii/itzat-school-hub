Privacy and advertising

Updated 16 October 2026.

Your browser preferences

Schedule Assistant stores your chosen school view, greeting name, class, theme, tutorial progress, ad preference and cached public timetable on your browser. Signed-in sessions are stored by Supabase. Saving a browser preference does not give it authority to grant staff access.

Accounts and school data

Supabase stores account details and the information you submit, including profile details, chat content, teacher timetable uploads and staff requests. Access depends on the feature and account permissions. Public school notices can be read without signing in. For account or data questions, contact the site Developer through the school. Do not put private student information in public notices or Q+A.

Usage measurement

The site uses Google Analytics to measure page visits. Its configuration excludes account and conversation data and disables Google signals and ad-personalization signals.

Optional advertising

Advertising helps cover hosting and bandwidth costs. The ad-amount choice is Off, Standard or More. Ads are intended only for desktop public school pages, with no mobile, popup or full-screen placements. Chat, profiles, staff administration and sign-in screens do not carry ads.

When advertising is activated, Google AdSense can use cookies and similar technologies for ad delivery, frequency limits, fraud prevention and reporting. Requests are configured for non-personalized ads. Non-personalized advertising can still use cookies; required consent controls must be configured before ad serving is activated.

You can turn off site ads in Settings or through the site's Close ad control. This stops further site ad requests and removes ad placements. It cannot undo data already sent to Google or remove cookies set by another domain. Your access to schedules and school updates stays the same.
